Built by someone who scopes the problem before the tool.

AiviaLabs started from a simple observation: most small businesses don't need more software, they need the software they already pay for to talk to each other. A CRM that doesn't hear from the website. A calendar nobody checks. A support inbox with a two-day lag.

Every engagement starts by mapping that one workflow — not by pitching a platform. If a five-line Zapier-style automation solves it, that's what ships. If it needs an AI agent making judgment calls, that's what ships instead.
